What I Can Do for Your Team

  • Business Analysis

    I evaluate current processes, uncover inefficiencies, and define clear requirements to help your business save money and build better products.

  • Reports and Dashboards

    I design Power BI and Excel reports that show you which products drive your sales. I also run root cause analysis, so you know why a problem is happening and how to fix it.

  • Stakeholder Management

    I agree with team leads on what should be measured, then explain the results in meetings and in training, so non-technical colleagues know what the numbers mean and what to do next.

  • Metric Definitions

    I agree and document standard definitions for key metrics, such as net profit and customer churn rate, so everyone on the team measures them the same way.

  • Market Research

    I study your customers, competitors, and prices, then turn the findings into a clear recommendation, so your pricing and product choices are based on the market.

  • Sales Forecast

    I use your past sales data to forecast what you are likely to sell next, so your business can plan purchases and set realistic targets.

  • Data Modelling

    I connect data from different sources, such as your CRM and databases, so you can find answers to business questions in one reliable view.

  • Data Automation

    I use Power Query, SQL, and Python to remove repeated manual work, so your reports refresh on their own and you can make decisions on time.

  • Data Cleaning

    I find and fix inconsistencies in your data, such as duplicates and wrong data types, that can lead to costly mistakes like overstated profits or undercounted customers.
